# Unguja location catalog

`unguja-landmarks.json` contains 2,500 searchable locations for Unguja. It combines named roads and destinations from OpenStreetMap with all 259 Unguja shehia, 7 districts, and 3 regions from the 2022 Tanzania National Bureau of Statistics ward shapefiles. Road ways use their OpenStreetMap center point; shehia selection points use the centroid of the NBS ward polygon.

OpenStreetMap data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), attribution at [openstreetmap.org/copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Administrative boundary source: [Tanzania National Bureau of Statistics 2022 PHC Ward Shapefiles](https://www.nbs.go.tz/index.php/statistics/topic/gis), cited as 2022 PHC, National Bureau of Statistics.

The 2,500 cap prioritizes every mapped street and administrative unit, then named settlements and public destinations. Unnamed roads and places absent from source mapping cannot be inferred from this catalog.
